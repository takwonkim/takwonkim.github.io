성신여자대학교 · 수리통계데이터사이언스학부

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations and mathematical finance, particularly regularity theory, free boundary problems, and stochastic control. I also work on statistical inference for high-dimensional data.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Seoul National University in 2022 under the supervision of Ki-Ahm Lee.

Research Interests

  • Option pricing and portfolio selection
  • Free boundary problems
  • Regularity theory and singularity analysis
  • Statistical testing for high-dimensional data
