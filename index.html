My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations and mathematical finance, particularly regularity theory, free boundary problems, and stochastic control.

In PDEs, I study regularity and boundary behavior for degenerate and singular parabolic equations. In mathematical finance, I study how constraints and irreversibility shape optimal consumption, investment, and retirement decisions.

Research Interests

  • Free boundary problems
  • Regularity theory and singularity analysis
  • Statistical testing for high-dimensional data
